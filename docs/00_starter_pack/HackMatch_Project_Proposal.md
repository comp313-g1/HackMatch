# Project Proposal — HackMatch

**Authors:** Team 1

## Overview

HackMatch is a platform designed to help students find suitable teammates before a hackathon begins. Hackathon teams are often formed quickly through Discord channels, social media, or in-person networking, which can result in teams with mismatched skills, experience levels, or goals.

HackMatch would allow participants to create developer profiles, browse upcoming hackathons, indicate that they are looking for a team, and receive recommended teammates based on complementary skills, availability, experience, and objectives.

## Executive Summary

### Problem Statement

Students attending hackathons frequently need to form teams in a short period of time. Current team formation often happens through crowded Discord channels or during the first part of an event. This makes it difficult for participants to evaluate whether prospective teammates have complementary technical skills, similar levels of commitment, or compatible goals.

### Proposed Solution

HackMatch would provide a centralized platform where students create profiles containing their skills, technology stack, experience level, school, goals, and links to previous work. Users could browse hackathons, post what type of teammate they need, receive recommended matches, invite other users, and create a confirmed team before the event begins.

## Project Overview

### Description

Core features could include:
- User registration and developer profiles
- Hackathon listings
- "Looking for Team" boards
- Skill-based teammate recommendations
- Team invitations and roster management

Later features could include in-app chat, project idea boards, post-hackathon reputation, and calendar integration.

#### In Scope

- Authentication and profile creation
- Hackathon listings
- Looking-for-team posts
- Rule-based teammate recommendations
- Team invitations and confirmed rosters

#### Out of Scope

- Full social-network functionality
- Advanced AI matching in the initial release
- Integrated video calls
- Full event-management tools

### Value Proposition

HackMatch could reduce the friction of last-minute team formation and help participants discover teammates outside their existing school or social circle. Its matching approach gives students a more structured way to form balanced teams before an event starts.

### Similar Products

Potential competitors or alternatives include:
- Devpost
- Major League Hacking community tools
- Discord hackathon servers
- LinkedIn
- General student networking platforms

### Differentiators

HackMatch focuses specifically on pre-hackathon team formation. Its main differentiator is matching participants based on complementary skills, goals, and availability rather than relying only on open chat channels or manual searching.
