# SmartSched

## Overview

SmartSched is an AI scheduling app for teams and campus collaboration. A user can type a simple request like, "Find a 45-minute slot for the capstone team this Thursday," and the system automatically builds the best meeting time.

## Key Features

- Chat-to-schedule: users describe the meeting in plain language
- Automatic calendar matching: checks attendees' availability from connected calendars
- Weighted prioritization: required members, such as leads or professors, are prioritized over optional attendees
- Focus-aware scheduling: reduces awkward gaps and clusters meetings more efficiently
- Campus-aware planning: accounts for walking time between buildings and room availability

## Why It Is Different

Most scheduling tools, including Calendly, use an all-or-nothing approach: if one person is unavailable, the system often says no time works. The usual workaround is a meeting poll, which slows everything down and depends on everyone responding.

SmartSched works differently. It uses weighted scheduling logic instead of strict overlap-only rules. It checks calendars directly, gives more importance to required people, and picks the strongest option automatically without waiting for manual voting.

## Value

SmartSched saves time, reduces scheduling friction, and helps campus teams coordinate more effectively. It is designed for student groups, project teams, faculty meetings, and any collaboration where location and availability matter.
